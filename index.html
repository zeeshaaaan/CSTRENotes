BPSC TRE Exam Overview

📋 Exam Pattern (Class 11–12 PGT CS)

  • Total Questions: 150 MCQs (150 Marks)
  • Part I (Language Qualifying): 30 Questions (English & Hindi)
  • Part II (General Studies): 40 Questions (Maths, Reasoning, GK, CA)
  • Part III (Computer Science Core): 80 Questions (In-depth CS)
  • Duration: 2 Hours 30 Minutes

📚 CS Syllabus Areas

  • Data Structures & Algorithms
  • Digital Electronics & Logic Design
  • Database Management Systems (DBMS)
  • Computer Networks & Internet
  • Operating Systems
  • Software Engineering
  • Computer Organization & Architecture
  • Programming (C, C++, Java basics)

🎯 Preparation Strategy

  • Focus on NCERT + standard CS textbooks
  • Practice MCQs from each topic daily
  • Use interactive tools to visualize concepts
  • Revise formulas, theorems, and algorithms regularly
  • Solve previous year BPSC TRE papers
  • Give timed mock tests weekly
